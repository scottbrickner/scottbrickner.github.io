Curriculum · ECG ECG Element Library — Registered Nurse Fourteen short interactive elements: rhythm identification, timed recognition drills, digital calipers, ACLS intervention sequences and escalation decisions. Updated November 2026 Spaced series · Microlearning example Microlearning, Built — AF with RVR A worked example from the "TL;DR" microlearning talk: watch one bloated 12-minute module become ten real, single-objective micro-units — branching scenarios, confidence-rated retrieval, one-minute wonders, job aids — then step into the live ECG Element Library. Open the old module for the before-and-after. Updated November 2026 Just-in-time unit · Microlearning example Microlearning, Built — Preventing Post-op AF One objective, about five minutes, for the start of a POD 0–1 CVICU shift: sort POAF prevention measures by their evidence, match each drug to what you watch, make two bedside calls, then retrieve with confidence ratings.
